Frequently Asked Questions About Our Free Invoice Generator

Everything you need to know about creating invoices online for free.

Is this invoice generator truly free?

Yes, InvoiceCraft is 100% free to use forever. You can generate unlimited professional invoices with no watermarks, no ads on PDFs, and absolutely no hidden charges or subscriptions.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No signup is required. Simply open the tool, fill in your details, and download your invoice as a PDF instantly. Your data is auto-saved in your browser for next time.

Can I create a GST invoice for free?

Yes. Our free invoice generator supports GST, VAT, CGST, SGST and custom tax labels. You can add HSN/SAC codes per line item and generate fully GST-compliant invoices for Indian businesses.

Can I add my company logo to the invoice?

Yes. Upload your company logo directly from the settings panel. It appears on all invoice templates and in the final PDF download — making your invoice look truly professional.

Is my invoice data secure and private?

Completely. All your data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage — nothing is ever sent to our servers. Your financial and client information stays 100% private.

How do I download the invoice as a PDF?

Click the "Share" button in the top-right of the invoice preview and select "Download PDF". Your invoice will be generated and downloaded instantly as a pixel-perfect PDF file.

What currencies does the invoice generator support?

InvoiceCraft supports USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, and more. Change the currency from the Calculations settings panel — amounts format automatically.

How many invoice templates are available?

There are 7 professional invoice templates: Modern, Bold, Classic, Minimal, Industrial, Indigo, and Basic. Each template is fully customizable with your brand color, font color, and logo.

Can I add discounts and taxes to my invoice?

Yes. Add percentage or fixed discounts, set custom tax rates (GST/VAT), apply rounding rules, and track amount paid vs balance due. Everything calculates automatically.

Can I send my invoice via WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the Share button to send your invoice directly via WhatsApp or email. On mobile, it shares the PDF directly; on desktop, it generates a WhatsApp link with your invoice summary.
